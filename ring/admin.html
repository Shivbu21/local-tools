Real auto-send: provider hookup

Honest note: a missed call can't trigger a browser on its own. Real auto-send needs a telephony provider (Exotel / Knowlarity) to detect the missed call, plus WhatsApp Business API to send the reply. The simulator + one-tap send above work today with no provider. Full contract and sample server in HOOK.md.

What the provider sends you

On every missed call, Exotel / Knowlarity POST to your webhook URL:

FieldMeaning
FromCaller's phone number
ToYour missed-call number
timestampWhen the call came in (ISO)

Setup checklist

      

        1. Get a virtual number from Exotel / Knowlarity and point its missed-call webhook at your server URL.

        2. Deploy the sample handler (any VPS / Render / Railway).

        3. Connect WhatsApp Business API (Meta) for the reply number.

        4. Paste the same template from above into the handler's TEMPLATE constant.

        5. Give a missed call to your number — the reply lands on WhatsApp automatically.
